Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Rodgers Plumbing & Drainage ("we", "us", "David Rodgers") is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to this website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is stored.

What information we collect

When you submit an enquiry through the contact form on this website, we collect your name, email address, suburb, and any details you provide in your message. This information is provided voluntarily by you.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics tools on this website.

How we use your information

The information you submit via the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry and arrange plumbing services. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party processing

Contact form submissions are processed and forwarded to us via Make.com (formerly Integromat), a third-party automation platform. Make.com may temporarily store your submission data on servers located outside Australia. For information on Make.com's privacy practices, see their Privacy Notice.

This website also embeds an Instagram feed widget provided by Behold. Behold may set cookies or collect data in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Data retention

We retain contact form enquiries only as long as necessary to respond to and fulfil your request. We do not maintain a marketing database.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us directly at david@rodgers-plumbing.com.au.
